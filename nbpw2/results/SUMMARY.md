# NBPW-2 pilot results (2026-11-06)

All results are generated by the scripts in this directory. Python 3.12,
numpy 2.2.6, CPU only. DMQA: 300 train / 100 test HotpotQA questions, 5 nodes,
100 distractors per node, local top-5, workspace K=5.

- Counterfactual mean: node 0/1 **0.60**, others ~0.

- 18/20 questions have non-zero credit.

**Reading**: both methods identify the gold-holding nodes, but counterfactual

## Decision

MVNB gate: network > best node (yes, 0.55 vs 0.00), but network > compute-matched
flat ensemble by >=5% (no, +2%). **Do not scale to 50 nodes yet.** Next:
sequential/active evidence gathering + dense retriever + larger test set.

## Revised decision

- One-shot workspace reranking: weak (+0.01–0.02); verifier is the lever.
- **Active evidence selection: strong** (+0.22–0.24 both@5 over random at B=2/3),
  replicates on 250 questions, and remains positive in a 10-node simulation.
- Predictive coding: still not at 2x / <=2% loss; keep as a tunable, not a win.
- Next: **10-node LAN** with real latency/loss; improve node profiles and try a
  dense retriever; 50-node WAN still premature.

## E11: learned node scorer (P1) — 1000-question dataset, 250 test

Replaces the hand-crafted node profile with a small logistic scorer over
node-level features (title gain, content gain, Jaccard, strong-title count,
reached/position, reliability, load), trained on 400 training questions with
"query every node" rollouts.

Numbers below are `NBPW2_RECALL=bm25` (reproducible offline). The default
`dense` path is wired through `nbpw2/dense.py` (bge-small-en-v1.5) and is
opt-in; it needs the model download and is not reproduced here.

| Budget | Policy | both@2 | both@5 | recall@5 | MRR | bytes |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2 | random | 0.041 | 0.095 | 0.377 | 0.548 | 5,537 |
| 2 | profile heuristic | 0.209 | 0.288 | 0.615 | 0.874 | 5,376 |
| 2 | **learned scorer** | **0.292** | **0.400** | **0.677** | **0.892** | **5,430** |
| 2 | oracle | 0.525 | 0.819 | 0.909 | 0.957 | 5,347 |
| 3 | random | 0.111 | 0.191 | 0.503 | 0.691 | 8,258 |
| 3 | profile heuristic | 0.292 | 0.387 | 0.673 | 0.887 | 8,204 |
| 3 | **learned scorer** | **0.375** | **0.496** | **0.734** | **0.910** | **8,126** |
| 3 | oracle | 0.481 | 0.672 | 0.833 | 0.945 | 8,180 |

Paired bootstrap, learned − profile at B=2: **+0.108, 95% CI [0.040, 0.180]**,
n=250.

**Reading**: the node scorer is now the binding constraint, exactly as the
11-09 audit predicted. A learned scorer closes ~32% of the profile→oracle gap
(0.288→0.400 of a 0.531 gap) at B=2 and ~40% at B=3, at the same byte budget. Note that oracle both@5 at
B=3 (0.672) is *lower* than at B=2 (0.819) because of candidate dilution in the
workspace; don't raise the budget blindly.

## Updated decision

- Selector is the bottleneck; better node scoring is the highest-leverage lever
  (E11: +10 points at B=2, statistically clear).
- Next: feed richer node signals (BM25 scores per node, dense-retrieval
  similarity, topic sharding) into the scorer; then a real 10-node LAN run.
